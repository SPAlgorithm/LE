TITLE (71 characters)
Build Any Number From Prime Quadruplets (Ladhe's Quad Conjecture, Part 2)

DESCRIPTION
Give me any whole number and I can build it from prime quadruplets using nothing but plus, minus and times. With the royal quad 2, 3, 5, 7 and the first quadruplet 11, 13, 17, 19 you can already write every integer from 0 to 156, for example 53 = 19 + 17 + 13 + 7 - 3 and 76 = 19 + 17 + 13 + 11 + (3*7) - 5. Each new quadruplet extends the range, and a two-line lemma proves the ladder never breaks. This is the hands-on half of Ladhe's Quad Conjecture: the rules, worked examples you can check in your head, the proof idea, and the LC tool's -d switch that derives any number or range for you.

Invented by Shubham Ladhe, Saloni Ladhe and Pankaj Ladhe.

Companion iOS app (LE-Games, free): https://apps.apple.com/app/id6767880385
Paper on Zenodo: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22286847
Paper, tool (LC) and dataset: https://github.com/SPAlgorithm/LE (folder LC)
